## 5. Human control and limitations

During the initial pilot, Leadmap collects a callback request. It does not independently confirm arrival time, booking, price, diagnosis, job acceptance, or emergency response. The Customer makes those decisions and remains responsible for its services and customer follow-up.

Leadmap does not guarantee call volume, booked jobs, revenue, savings, or uninterrupted availability. The safety promise covers the controlled approval process, not commercial results.

The Customer is responsible for its legal basis and caller-facing information unless the parties document another allocation.
