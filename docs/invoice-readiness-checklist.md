# Invoice readiness checklist

> Verify with the company's accountant or invoice provider before issuing the first invoice. This checklist follows Skatteverket's general full-invoice fields but does not replace tax advice.

Review source: [Skatteverket — Momslagens regler om fakturering](https://www.skatteverket.se/foretagochorganisationer/moms/saljavarorochtjanster/fakturering.4.58d555751259e4d66168000403.html).

## Supplier configuration

- [ ] [LEGAL COMPANY NAME]
- [ ] [ORGANISATION NUMBER]
- [ ] [BUSINESS ADDRESS]
- [ ] [VAT NUMBER]
- [ ] F-tax status wording, if used
- [ ] Payment account, Bankgiro, or IBAN
- [ ] Invoice email and support contact
- [ ] Unique, continuous invoice-number series

## Customer configuration

- [ ] Customer legal name and address
- [ ] Customer organisation number
- [ ] Customer invoice email or e-invoice route
- [ ] Purchase-order/reference field
- [ ] Customer VAT number when required

## Full invoice fields

- [ ] Invoice date
- [ ] Unique invoice number
- [ ] Supply/service date if different
- [ ] Clear service description and period
- [ ] Quantity/minutes and unit price where relevant
- [ ] Price excluding VAT by VAT rate
- [ ] Applicable VAT rate
- [ ] VAT amount
- [ ] Total excluding and including VAT
- [ ] Due date and payment terms
- [ ] Contract or pilot reference

## Leadmap line-item template

1. `Leadmap VVS Pilot — monthly service [PERIOD]` — 2,900 SEK excluding VAT.
2. `Leadmap managed setup` — 2,000 SEK excluding VAT.
3. `Usage above 500 included minutes — [MINUTES] × 2.50 SEK` when applicable.

Do not invoice Premium until the internal unit-economics check passes with the configured provider cost.
