# First VVS pilot sales playbook

## The offer

Sell one controlled, done-for-you VVS pilot. Do not sell "AI" as the outcome.

- Problem: the owner or dispatcher cannot answer every call while driving, working on site, or after closing.
- Outcome: every selected missed or after-hours call gets an approved answer, qualification, and a clear handoff.
- Pilot: 2,900 SEK/month plus 2,000 SEK setup, 500 minutes, seven pilot days, then 30 days' cancellation notice. All prices exclude VAT.
- Safety promise: no customer traffic is connected before the buyer has approved the voice, script, fallback rules, and handoff in writing.
- Scope boundary: Leadmap does not guarantee call volume, booked jobs, or revenue. During the pilot the VVS company confirms booking, price, priority, and delivery.

## Best first buyer

Prioritize Swedish VVS businesses where:

- the owner, service manager, or working supervisor still handles incoming calls;
- calls arrive during jobs, driving, lunch, or after closing;
- a missed urgent call can become a lost service job;
- the decision-maker can approve a test flow within one week;
- the company will call qualified handoffs back promptly.

Reject or defer businesses looking for consumer plumbing help, recruitment, a full call center, automatic price promises, or a guaranteed number of jobs.

## 30-minute demo agenda

1. **0-5 minutes — diagnose:** Ask when calls are missed, who answers now, and what happens after voicemail.
2. **5-10 minutes — show the call:** Run the Swedish VVS demo or the clearly labeled simulation.
3. **10-15 minutes — show the handoff:** Confirm the exact fields the owner wants: name, phone, location, issue, urgency, and preferred callback time.
4. **15-20 minutes — map the first flow:** Choose missed calls, after-hours calls, or both. Define unsafe topics and the human fallback.
5. **20-25 minutes — commercial fit:** Explain price, included minutes, setup, pilot length, and cancellation in one pass.
6. **25-30 minutes — close:** Ask for the pilot start decision and the person who will approve the script.

## Close language

Use a direct close:

> Det här verkar passa eftersom ni tappar samtal när ni är ute på jobb. Nästa steg är inte att koppla på allt. Vi bygger ett VVS-flöde, ni godkänner ett testsamtal och överlämningen, och först därefter startar de sju pilotdagarna. Ska vi sätta startdatumet nu?

If the buyer is interested but not ready, book one specific follow-up with one missing decision. Do not accept an undefined "hör av dig senare".

## Objection handling

### "Det låter robotiskt"

Show the live demo when production credentials are connected. Otherwise use the clearly labeled simulation and explain that the buyer approves the actual voice before any customer traffic is connected.

### "Vi kan bara svara själva"

Agree that the team should answer when available. Position Leadmap only for the calls they already miss or cannot take after closing.

### "Vi vill inte byta nummer"

They keep their number. The pilot forwards only the agreed missed or after-hours calls.

### "Kan den boka jobb automatiskt?"

Not in the initial pilot. It collects a qualified callback request. The VVS company confirms time, price, priority, and delivery.

### "Kan ni garantera fler jobb?"

No. Guarantee the controlled start process, not demand or revenue. Use the buyer's real pilot data to decide whether the service should continue.

### "Det är för dyrt"

Do not discount immediately. Compare the full monthly cost with the buyer's own average job value and missed-call volume using the user-controlled calculator. If the buyer still sees no economic fit, disqualify them.

## First-pilot proof capture

Agree in writing before launch that anonymized operational data may be used only after buyer approval. Capture:

- selected call window and approved scope;
- number of calls Leadmap handled;
- number of qualified callback requests;
- number requiring manual review;
- response time from handoff to owner action, when available;
- buyer-approved qualitative feedback;
- any failures, corrections, or unsafe cases;
- whether the buyer continued after the pilot.

Never publish customer identity, audio, transcript excerpts, percentages, revenue, or testimonials without explicit written approval and a traceable source.

## Daily pilot QA

During the seven pilot days:

1. Review every summary and every fallback flag.
2. Check that no booking, price, or arrival time was promised without authority.
3. Correct the script when the same misunderstanding appears twice.
4. Notify the buyer immediately about any safety or routing failure.
5. Send one short end-of-pilot scorecard with facts, limitations, and the continue/stop recommendation.

## Exit criteria

Recommend continuation only when the system handled the agreed scope safely and the buyer confirms that the handoffs were useful. Stop or re-scope when the buyer does not answer qualified handoffs, the call volume is too low to evaluate, or the flow repeatedly requires information the AI should not provide.
